Set up your sign-in

One-time setup. Pick a password you’ll use from now on and paste your GitHub access key once — the key is encrypted with your password and saved. After this, you’ll only ever need your password.

Get a key ↗
Choose a strong password. Your key is encrypted before it’s stored, but the encrypted file sits in a public repo — a weak password could be guessed. The key only grants edit access to this one site, and you can revoke it anytime on GitHub.
Anything you publish goes into a public site — uploaded documents and posts are publicly viewable. Signing in protects who can edit, not who can view.
